Privacy

Noctune runs on your computer. It has no account system, sends no analytics and no crash reports.

What stays on your computer

Your library index, playlists, settings, cover images and background picture are saved in Noctune's data folder. Your music files are only read, never changed or uploaded.

What leaves your computer

Only what you turn on: signing in to Spotify or SoundCloud talks to those services; an Apple Music token is checked with Apple; a linked YouTube video loads from YouTube; community lyrics, when switched on, send the track title and artist to LRCLIB. Sign-in tokens are kept in your system keychain.

This website

The site uses no cookies and no trackers. The demo in the page saves its settings in your browser's local storage.
