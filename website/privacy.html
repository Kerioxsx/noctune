Privacy

Last updated 28 November 2026 (lyrics and official videos are now found automatically)

Noctune is a music player that runs on your computer. It has no accounts of its own and no servers, it shows no ads, and it sends no analytics, usage statistics or crash reports. Nobody involved in making Noctune receives any of your data.

What stays on your computer

Noctune keeps these in its own data folder on your computer and nowhere else:

  • the list of music folders and files you add, and an index of their titles, artists, albums and lengths
  • cover art read from your files, and cover pictures or backgrounds you choose (copies, so moving the originals is fine)
  • your playlists, your display name and avatar, your settings and the last thing you were playing
  • lyrics files you import, and lyrics and official-video links found online for your songs (saved so they work offline)
  • sign-in tokens for Spotify or SoundCloud, encrypted with your system's keychain (Keychain on macOS, Credential protection on Windows, GNOME Keyring or KWallet on Linux). If no keychain is available, Noctune does not store them at all.

Your music files are only read. Noctune never changes, moves, deletes or uploads them.

What leaves your computer, and when

Only when you use one of these features. Each goes straight from your computer to that service, under that service's own privacy policy.

FeatureSent toWhat is sent
Connecting SpotifySpotifyYou sign in on Spotify's own page in your browser; Noctune never sees your password. Noctune then reads your saved albums and playlists and sends play, pause, seek and volume commands.
Connecting SoundCloudSoundCloudThe same: sign-in on SoundCloud's page, then reading your likes and playlists and streaming the tracks you play.
Adding an Apple Music tokenAppleThe developer token you paste, to check that it works.
Finding lyrics (on by default; Settings → Settings → Find lyrics online)LRCLIB (lrclib.net)The title, artist, album and length of a song, once, to look up its timed lyrics.
Finding official videos (on by default; Settings → Settings → Play official music videos)MusicBrainz (musicbrainz.org)The title and artist of a song, once, to find the official video its editors have linked.
Playing an official videoYouTube (youtube-nocookie.com)The video's ID when it plays. The player is YouTube's own, so YouTube receives your IP address like any embedded video.
Updates (automatic)GitHubA few times a day Noctune asks GitHub whether a newer version exists, and downloads it when you choose Update. GitHub sees your IP address and the app's version.

Noctune does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

  • In Noctune: Settings → Account → Delete all my data erases everything above and restarts the app. Disconnecting a service in Settings → Connections removes its sign-in from your computer.
  • After uninstalling, the data folder can stay behind. Delete it to remove everything: ~/Library/Application Support/Noctune on macOS, %APPDATA%\Noctune on Windows, ~/.config/Noctune on Linux.
  • To revoke access at the service too, remove Noctune from the connected apps in your Spotify or SoundCloud account settings.

Settings → Account → Export saves your playlists, profile and settings as a file you can keep or move.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The live demo on the home page saves its settings in your browser's local storage, which you can clear in your browser at any time. Download buttons fetch the installers from GitHub.

Children

Noctune collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If what Noctune stores or sends changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed. For questions or requests, open an issue at github.com/kerioxsx/noctune/issues.
